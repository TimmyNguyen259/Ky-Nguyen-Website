VNGGames is the game business within VNG, a Vietnamese technology company. Reusing “VNG Fresher” for its early-career program would have made recruitment easier. It would also have kept the program anchored to the parent-company category.

The room defaulted to the safer name. The reasoning was familiar: awareness is expensive, a recognized brand shortens the funnel, and launching a new identity into the same graduate market forces you to build attention from zero.

The counter-argument was not aesthetic. VNGGames needed a young-talent proposition built around making games, not a renamed corporate intake. A shared name would keep pulling the applicant profile back toward the parent-company category — the same profile the program was trying to move away from.

So we chose a separate identity — **VNGGames Nextgen** — and accepted the burden of building awareness from zero. The program hit its intake target. More importantly, the decision proved that sharper positioning can change who sees themselves in the opportunity.

> Build the brand for the talent you need, not the brand you already have.
